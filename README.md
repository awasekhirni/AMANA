# AMANA
Agricultural Market Access Network Alliance(AMANA)
- From farm to family. Halal. Direct. Fair
- The trust ring around your food.

  A community-driven group-buying platform that aggregates household capital to purchase wholesale directly from
  local farmers, bypassing retail intermediaries to reduce food costs by 60-70% while stabilizing farmer income.


  Core friction: Existing retail food supply chain extracts 60-75% of the final consumer price through middlemen or intermediation,
  logistics fragmentation, and spoilage, while farmers capture a shrinking share and consumers face unsustainable grocery inflation.

  cost of inaction:
  a family of 4-5 people spends around $1200/month on groceries could have saved around 7200 to 10,000 annually by shifting to group-buy pricing.

farmers need a direct-to-consumer group buying platform that can increase their farm-gate revenue by 20-40% by eliminating distributor margins.
Most importantly, farmers should have mechanism to cold-storage the produce. Furthermore, their reliance on industrial retail supply chains perpetuates 
atleast 30-40% foodwaste as per insights from FAO baseline.
